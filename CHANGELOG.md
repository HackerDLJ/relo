# Changelog

## 0.3.1 (2026-11-04)
- Glassmorphism and liquid-glass styling: frosted cards, bars and dialogs over a soft colour field, refractive floating bottom nav, glossy buttons. Falls back to solid surfaces when blur is unsupported or reduced transparency is requested.
- Fixed the clipped logo in the mobile header.

## 0.3.0 (2026-11-04)
- Selling and refurbished listings: rent, sell or both, refurbished grade and warranty, buy offers in a private chat, Mark as sold. No payments are processed.
- Discover filters: Borrow or rent, Buy, Refurbished.
- Real product photos (supplied photos plus credited Wikimedia Commons images) on every listing.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-04)
- Real accounts and shared database on Supabase, deployed on Vercel.
- Google and phone sign-in, light/dark/system theme, per-day pricing and private rent negotiation chat.

## 0.1.0
- First local-first prototype with the matching, ranking, waitlist and trust engine.
